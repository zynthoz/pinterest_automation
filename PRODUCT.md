# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Affiliate marketers and content creators who need to automate the curation and scheduling of Amazon products to Pinterest. The user operates this in a focused, workflow-oriented context.

## Product Purpose
A personal content management and scheduling dashboard that connects a Pinterest Business account to queue and schedule product pins. It extracts Amazon products, finds matching TikTok videos, generates AI copy, and publishes them securely.

## Brand Personality
Efficient, reliable, clean, and utilitarian. It should feel like a professional work surface, not a flashy marketing site.

## Anti-references
- Busy, overstimulating SaaS dashboards with too many gradient metrics.
- Flashy consumer apps.
- Sites with generic "AI" branding (purple/blue gradients, glowing borders).

## Design Principles
- **Workflow First**: Optimize for repetitive data entry (pasting links) and clear state visibility.
- **Utilitarian Clarity**: Avoid unnecessary decoration; let the data and job status be the focus.
- **Confident and Calm**: Errors and rate limits happen; surface them calmly and clearly without alarm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility (WCAG AA). Clear contrast for job status colors (red/green/yellow). Form inputs must have clear labels and error states.
